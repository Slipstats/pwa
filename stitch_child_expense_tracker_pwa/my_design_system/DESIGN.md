# Design System

## Brand & Style
The design system adopts a clean, professional, and modern corporate aesthetic focused on clarity, reliability, and precision. It utilizes the **Inter** font family for all typographical hierarchies, ensuring crisp readability across all digital surfaces.

## Colors
- **Primary**: `#1275e2`
- **Secondary**: `#5f78a3`
- **Tertiary**: `#c55b00`
- **Neutral**: `#74777f`

## Typography
- **Font Family**: Inter (Headline, Body, and Label)

## Layout & Spacing
- **Spacing Scale**: Level 2 (Modular rhythm based on clean base units)

## Elevation & Depth
- Flat-yet-layered structure relying on clean surface contrast and subtle borders.

## Shapes
- **Roundedness**: Soft (`1`), providing gentle, approachable corners across containers and interactive elements.

## Components
- Standardized buttons, inputs, and cards utilizing the core color palette and soft rounding.
